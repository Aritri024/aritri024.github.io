Project

Fluid-Structure Interaction at Re = 250

Parallel C/C++ simulation and wake-flow analysis on HPC.

← Back to selected projects

Overview

At Jadavpur University’s Microfluidics Lab, I developed and executed parallel simulations to study unsteady fluid-structure interaction involving a cylinder and pitching flat plate.

Problem

The study examined how controlled operating parameters affected unsteady wake behavior, vorticity, and flow separation at Re = 250.

Approach

  • Developed and executed parallel C/C++ simulations on an HPC system.
  • Compared response across Strouhal number and pitching phase.
  • Used Tecplot to quantify wake dynamics, vorticity, and flow separation.

Results

  • Identified sensitivity of system response to design and operating parameters.
