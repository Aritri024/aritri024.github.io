20 kW High-Temperature Furnace & Test Platform

About

About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Minnesota - Twin Cities with hands-on R&D experience in thermal-fluid systems, prototype development, test and validation, instrumentation, and data analysis. My current research at the T.E. Murphy Engine Lab includes development of a 20 kW high-temperature experimental platform, mechanical design in SolidWorks, automated flow and sensor control using NI cDAQ and LabVIEW, and characterization using FTIR, PIV, chemiluminescence, and computed tomography. My earlier work includes CFD studies of minichannel heat-sink performance and HPC simulations of fluid-structure interaction. Across these projects, I use experimental and computational methods to understand system behavior, improve performance, and translate test results into engineering decisions.
