About

Mechanical engineer and experimental researcher

I am a Mechanical Engineering Ph.D. candidate at the University of Minnesota working on high-temperature thermal-fluid systems and experimental engineering. My current research combines mechanical design and prototype development with process-gas delivery, instrumentation, automated controls, and optical diagnostics.

Across my projects, I work from physical system design through commissioning, measurement, troubleshooting, and quantitative analysis. My earlier research includes CFD-based thermal-management studies and HPC simulations of fluid-structure interaction.
