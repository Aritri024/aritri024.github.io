Project

20 kW High-Temperature Furnace & Test Platform

Mechanical design, instrumentation, automated control, experimental diagnostics, and performance validation.

← Back to selected projects

Overview

Designed and validated a 20 kW experimental furnace and modular test hardware for controlled thermal-fluid testing and engineering evaluation.

Engineering Approach

SolidWorks models, engineering drawings, GD&T, tolerance stack-ups, DFM/DFA, fit and clearance analysis, BOM development, and high-temperature materials selection were used to translate the experimental concept into manufacturable hardware.

Instrumentation & Test Integration

The experimental platform integrated ammonia, methane, and air delivery with mass flow controllers, pressure and temperature sensing, NI cDAQ hardware, and LabVIEW for automated control and synchronized data acquisition.

Experimental Methods

A four-variable Design of Experiments was combined with FTIR, Particle Image Velocimetry, chemiluminescence imaging, and computed-tomography measurements to characterize operating conditions and system performance.

Results

Testing produced repeatable process data and engineering recommendations in collaboration with CenterPoint Energy and the University of Minnesota Duluth. The work reported a 60% reduction in NOx compared with conventional burners.
